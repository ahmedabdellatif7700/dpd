Step 3: the resampler 50 <-> 8 samples per symbol (dpd/resample.py) and the DPD through it

1. filter: 503 taps at 80 GS/s, passband (to 1.2 GHz) ripple 0.0017 dB, stopband (from 2.0 GHz) -80.3 dB
2. the formula against scipy.signal.upfirdn: largest difference 1.9e-15
3. 50 -> 8 against the waveform made at 8: gain 2.49990 (sqrt(50/8) = 2.5, the RRC's), error -68.7 dB, no delay
4. round trip 50 -> 8 -> 50: error -70.8 dB on the whole drive, -35.3 dB with each 6400-sample frame alone
